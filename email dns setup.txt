Let's get your mail going to the right place
Before we can finish setting up your account, we need to verify that you own amanicrafts.com.

Log in to your account with your current DNS host. What is DNS?

Set the following TXT records.

Name	TXT Value
@	T9871909
@	v=spf1 include:secureserver.net -all
_dmarc	v=DMARC1; p=reject; rua=mailto:dmarc_rua@onsecureserver.net; adkim=r; aspf=r;
CNAME Records: Find where to add a CNAME record and enter:

Host	Points to
email	email.secureserver.net
secureserver1._domainkey	s1.dkim.amanicrafts_com.2f5.onsecureserver.net.
secureserver2._domainkey	s2.dkim.amanicrafts_com.2f5.onsecureserver.net.
MX Records: Find where to add an MX record and enter:

Host	Priority	Target
@	0	smtp.secureserver.net
@	10	mailstore1.secureserver.net
SRV Records: Find where to add an SRV record and enter:

Port	Protocol	Name	Service	Priority	Weight	Target
443	_tcp	@	_autodiscover	100	1	autodiscover.secureserver.net
If you have existing email destination records on your domain, you need to delete the old records before your Professional Email will work. Any changes you make to your records can take up to 24 hours to take effect.
